## 📖 About The Project

In space, astronauts can't visit a doctor. They face unique hazards like **microgravity, radiation, isolation, and dust** that affect bone, muscle, heart, brain, and vision.

**AURA** translates NASA's Human Research Program science into a simple, visual tool that lets any astronaut check their health, understand the risk, and know exactly what to do next.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 👨‍🚀 Clickable Human Body Map
Click **Brain, Heart, Lungs, Muscle, Bone, Skin** to see:
- Function of the organ
- How spaceflight affects it
- Treatment & prevention steps

### 2. 📊 Live Vitals Dashboard
Enter:
- Heart Rate (bpm)
- SpO2 (%)
- Temperature (°C)
- Blood Sugar (mg/dL)
- Blood Pressure
- Sleep (Hours)

Get instant **Green 🟢 / Yellow 🟡 / Red 🔴** color coding.

### 3. 🩺 AI Health Guidance
Instant assessment:
- 🟢 **GO** - Fit for EVA / Mission tasks
- 🟡 **CAUTION** - Light duty only, monitor
- 🔴 **NO-GO** - Contact Flight Surgeon Now

Plus personalized treatment advice.

### 4. 📈 Dynamic Charts
7-day trend for Heart Rate, Blood Sugar, and Sleep using **Chart.js**

### 5. 🎮 Gamification
- XP Points, Streaks, Levels
- Progress Bar
- Badges: Strong Crew, Sharp Mind, Perfect Day

### 6. 🌍 5 Environments
- 🛰️ Space Station - ISS
- 🚀 Spacecraft - Orion
- 🌕 Lunar Base - Artemis
- 🔴 Mars Base
- 🚙 Rover - EVA

### 7. 📸 Image Upload
Upload Ultrasound / Skin images for quick AI check.

### 8. 🚨 Emergency Button
One-click alert to Flight Surgeon with location + vitals.
